HOW TO PLAY

Four steps to fast fingers

Every lesson is a quick battle. Typing the right keys powers your hero.

STEP 1

Pick a hero

Choose a buddy, dress them up in the Hero Closet, and set off across Keyloria.

STEP 2

Type to zap

Fingers home on F and J. Each right key zaps a Scrambler. Colors show which finger to use.

STEP 3

Befriend Keylori

Win a stage and a Keylori joins your collection. There are over 100 to find.

STEP 4

Evolve and level up

Keep practicing and your Keylori evolve. Stars, diamonds and trophies track progress.

ARCADE

Eight games for extra practice

Short, replayable games with high scores for every player on the device.
