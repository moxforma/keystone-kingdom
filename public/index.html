HOW TO PLAY

Four steps to fast fingers

Every lesson is a quick battle. Typing the right keys powers your hero, and true fun facts make every sentence worth reading.

STEP 1

Pick a hero

Choose a buddy, dress them up in the Hero Closet, and set off across Keyloria.

STEP 2

Type to zap

Fingers home on F and J. Each right key zaps a Scrambler. Colors show which finger to use.

STEP 3

Befriend Keylori

Win a stage and a Keylori joins your collection. There are over 100 to find.

STEP 4

Evolve and level up

Keep practicing and your Keylori evolve. Stars, diamonds and trophies show progress.

ARCADE

Eight games for extra practice

Short, replayable games with high scores for every player on the device, from easy all the way to Beast mode.

MULTIPLAYER

Race your friends live

Host a race, share the link, and everyone types the same fun fact at the same time, each as their own hero.

  • Host a race and get a short room code, or tap Copy invite link to send it.
  • Up to 40 racers in one room, each shown with their own hero and outfit.
  • Five difficulty levels, from Easy to Impossible.
  • Rematch with one tap: everyone goes straight back to the race room.
  • Friend challenges: share your arcade score and a friend races your ghost.

SAFE BY DESIGN

Built for young typists, with grown-ups in control.

First names only

Only a first name and typing stats are shared with a class. No emails, no passwords, no profiles.

No chat

Kids can't message each other or strangers. Races show names and progress, nothing else.

Focus mode

Parents and teachers can turn off the arcade, Hero Closet, catch and evolve animations, the Keylori walking on the menu and more, so it is strictly a learning game. Teachers can lock it for the whole class.

Grown-up check

Settings and teacher tools sit in Teachers, behind a quick grown-up check, and only the class creator can see the dashboard.
